Cold start questions

The suggestions Piper shows above the composer before the conversation gets going: ColdStartQuestions, “Ask me things like:” and up to the admin’s limit of questions (three by default). Picking one sends it, and the next unused question takes its place; the suggestions hide once the visitor has sent more than three messages, when the messenger is collapsed, or when the visitor closes them. It's markup only, with no Piper running. Each state is captured from fireball by rendering the whole messenger (Main); the “after a pick” states come from the state the component saves, as a returning visitor’s would. Each frame is its own viewport. Open one on its own to use DevTools on it.

What to check

  • Quiet by design: a small muted “Ask me things like:” with a close button, and the questions as plain muted lines that darken on hover, not buttons.
  • The limit: three by default; five when the admin allows it, which takes noticeably more of the panel.
  • Long questions wrap to the panel’s width, one under another.
  • After a pick: the picked question is in the timeline, and the next unused one has taken its place. With three picked, only the last two are left.
  • With CTA buttons: the strategy buttons sit between the suggestions and the composer.
  • Focus: Tab to a question for the theme’s primary-colour outline.

Refresh the capture after fireball changes: npm run cold-start:refresh. See the README.
