Composer

Where the visitor types: ChatComposer and ChatInputForm (the TextArea, the send and voice buttons, the strategy CTA buttons and the character count) and ComposerErrorBanner. It's markup only, with no Piper running and nothing behind the buttons. Each state is captured from fireball by rendering the whole messenger (Main) and typing into the real textarea where there’s text, in an open shadow root on #qualified-multimodal-host with production’s host style. The frame repeats two things the composer does with real layout: it sizes a typed textarea to its content, and it measures the placeholder, switching to the shorter fallback when the ideal one doesn’t fit, as useTextOverflow does. Each frame is its own viewport. Open one on its own to use DevTools on it.

What to check

  • Placeholders: “Ask Bob Llama a question” before the visitor engages, the admin’s own text when set, and “Ask a question…” when the ideal one doesn’t fit (the admin’s at 320).
  • Typing: the send button turns on with text; several lines grow the box up to its cap, then it scrolls.
  • Over the limit: a red “2,063 / 2,000 characters” under the box, and send stays off.
  • Errors: a failed send shows the banner under the composer. A rejected email reply puts the text back, outlines the box in red and says “Invalid email address”; the email ask itself only changes the placeholder.
  • CTA buttons: a row above the composer when open, stacked full width on the collapsed card.
  • Voice mode: the mic (“Speak with Bob Llama”) sits next to send.

Refresh the capture after fireball changes: npm run composer:refresh. See the README.
