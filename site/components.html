Meeting booker: Before (QAI-6749) After

Components

Real Qualified components, styled by a real Qualified stylesheet. This is the PiperX full-screen meeting booker (LightboxExperience → MeetingTimeSelector), with no routing, rep or calendar. The markup is captured from fireball by rendering the real components. Switch between the original QAI-6749 bug and the shipped fix. Each frame is its own viewport, so the app's mobile media queries (≤ 767px) apply. Open one frame on its own to use DevTools on it.

What to check

  • The bug (QAI-6749), from code without the fix (320 to 414): four time columns, and "9:00 AM" wraps to two lines and spills past the 36px pill. The day strip has arrows on both sides, squeezing the seven day cards.
  • After (three columns, one-line pills): below 768px, three columns, the arrows beside the month title, and every label on one line at 320, 375 and 414. At 768 and wider, the layout keeps four columns.
  • Focus: Tab into a frame. Day cards and time pills should lift on :focus-visible. Clicking a time selects it (aria-pressed).
